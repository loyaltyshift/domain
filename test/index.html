Good to know

A few questions, answered.

Not sure which option fits? Start with a short conversation and we’ll work out the smallest useful next step.

Is this a fully automated AI service?

Not necessarily. The aim is to automate repeatable collection and summarisation where practical, while keeping human judgement in the loop for interpretation and recommendations. The exact level of automation depends on the sources and scope.

What kind of businesses is LoyaltyShift for?

We’re initially focused on customer-facing businesses that want a more structured view of feedback and customer journeys. The best fit depends on the volume and accessibility of your customer signals.

Do you need access to our customer database?

Not for a public-data snapshot. Any work involving private customer data or connected systems would be scoped separately, with appropriate permissions, security and privacy safeguards.

Can you promise more loyalty or revenue?

No responsible review can promise a specific outcome. We aim to identify evidence-based opportunities and practical improvements, then help you decide what to test and measure.
