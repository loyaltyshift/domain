The Intelligence

Loyalty Shift Index (LSI)

Static NPS metrics tell you what a customer feels today, but not how that feeling is evolving. Our Loyalty Shift Index (LSI) quantifies the directional movement of sentiment, acting as an early-warning radar for your brand's retention strategy.

Predictive Churn Modeling

Using machine learning, we identify the customers quietly drifting from Promoters to Passives. By blending behavioral data with sentiment momentum, we pinpoint revenue at risk and uncover opportunities for timely re-engagement.
